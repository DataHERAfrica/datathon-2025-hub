# datathon-2025-hub
The DataHER Africa Datathon 2025 brought together brilliant African women from across the continent to learn, collaborate, and build data-driven solutions addressing real social challenges.

Over two days of learning, teamwork, mentorship, and innovation, participants explored how AI and data analytics can drive impact in health, education, sustainability, and women’s empowerment.

This initiative continues our mission: To equip Black and Brown women with the skills, confidence, and community they need to thrive in data and AI careers.
